📊 Understanding Press Monitoring Metrics

💡 Quick Overview: This dashboard displays real-time performance data for all press machines. Metrics update automatically every 5 seconds, giving you live visibility into machine operations.

🔢 Stroke Count

What it is: The total number of press operations (strokes) completed by the machine since it started running.

Why it matters: This shows the cumulative production output of the press. Higher numbers indicate more work completed.

⚡ Stroke Rate

What it is: The average number of strokes the press completes per minute, calculated over a 10-minute window.

Why it matters: This indicates the current operating speed of the press. A higher rate means faster production.

Typical values: Normal production rates vary by press type, but sudden drops may indicate issues.

🌡️ Temperature

What it is: The current temperature reading from the press machine's sensor, measured in degrees Celsius (°C).

Why it matters: Monitors machine health. Excessive heat can indicate mechanical problems or need for maintenance.

  • Normal: Temperature is within safe operating range
  • Warning: Temperature is elevated, monitor closely
  • Critical: Temperature is too high, requires immediate attention

📳 Vibration

What it is: The vibration intensity measured in millimeters per second (mm/s), showing mechanical oscillations.

Why it matters: Excessive vibration can indicate wear, misalignment, or mechanical issues that need attention.

  • Normal: Vibration is within acceptable levels
  • Warning: Elevated vibration detected
  • Critical: Excessive vibration requires immediate inspection

📋 Latest Command

What it is: The most recent control command sent to the press machine, along with when it was issued.

Common commands:

  • START: Command to begin operations
  • STOP: Command to halt operations
  • CHANGE_SPEED: Adjust operating speed
  • ADJUST: Fine-tune machine parameters

🔴🟢 Connection Status

What it means:

  • 🟢 Green dot: Press is connected and sending data (last update within 20 minutes)
  • 🔴 Red dot: Press is disconnected or hasn't sent data in over 20 minutes

Why it matters: Ensures you're viewing current data. A disconnected press may need network or sensor troubleshooting.

🔄 Data Refresh: The page automatically refreshes live metrics every 5 seconds. You can also click the "🔄 Refresh" button for an immediate full page reload.
